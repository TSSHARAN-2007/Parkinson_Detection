Parkinson’s often changes how a hand moves, and that shows up in a simple drawing. Sketch a spiral or a wave, or upload one, and two trained models score it for tremor patterns.

From pen strokes to a score

Each drawing goes through the same steps the notebook used for training and testing.

Draw or upload

Trace the dashed spiral or wave, or upload a photo of a paper drawing.

Resize to 224 × 224

The image is scaled to the size EfficientNetB0 expects, in colour.

Score with the matching model

The spiral model and the wave model each output the chance of a Parkinson’s pattern.

Average the two

If you submit both drawings, their scores are averaged for a combined result.

Before you rely on this

This is a student research project trained on a small dataset. It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose Parkinson’s disease. If you are worried about tremor or movement changes, see a doctor.
